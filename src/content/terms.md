## 2. Description of Service

Ari Connect is a Discord bot that enables **cross-server communication** through a lobby-based webhook system. Connected channels in different servers can relay messages, events, polls, and moderation actions across all participating channels within the same lobby.

The Service includes:

- Cross-server message relay via Discord webhooks.
- Lobby creation and management tools.
- Moderation features including muting, filtering, and reporting.
- A companion web portal for analytics, event management, and dashboard access (availability subject to subscription tier).
